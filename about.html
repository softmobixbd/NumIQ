NumIQ is a daily math puzzle game built for anyone who loves a good mental workout — number sequences, equations, geometric patterns, and more, in bite-sized daily challenges.

1What is NumIQ?

NumIQ turns classic logic-puzzle formats into a daily habit. Every puzzle is quick to read and quick to attempt, but the patterns behind them range from "warm up your brain" simple to genuinely tricky — so there's always a reason to come back tomorrow.

No trivia, no reflex tests, no luck. Just numbers, patterns, and the satisfying click of spotting the rule.

3Track your progress

Every solve earns XP and coins, builds your streak, and nudges your IQ score. Unlock badges as you go, climb the leaderboard, and watch your accuracy improve over time — NumIQ keeps a running picture of how you're doing, not just today's score.

4NumIQ Pro

Free players get the full daily challenge and puzzle library. NumIQ Pro adds an ad-free experience, unlimited hints, 30 bonus Pro-only puzzles every day, early access to new puzzle types, and exclusive themes.
