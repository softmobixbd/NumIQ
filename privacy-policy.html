1Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how NumIQ ("we," "our," "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using NumIQ, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2Information we collect

Account information. When you play as a guest, we create an anonymous device account with no personal information attached. If you choose to link a Google account, we receive your Google display name, email address, and profile photo URL.

Gameplay data. We store your puzzle solves, streaks, XP, coin balance, badges, IQ score, difficulty preferences, and puzzle history so your progress carries across sessions and devices.

Push notification token. If you enable notifications, we store a device token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used only to send you gameplay-related reminders (daily challenge reminders, streak warnings).

Purchase information. If you buy NumIQ Pro or a coin pack, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made.

Advertising data. If you're on the free tier, our ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, subject to your device's ad tracking permissions.

3How we use your information

  • To operate core gameplay: puzzles, progress tracking, streaks, leaderboards, and Battle Mode
  • To personalize your daily puzzle difficulty and recommended feed
  • To send optional gameplay reminders, if notifications are enabled
  • To process and deliver in-app purchases
  • To serve and measure advertising for non-Pro users
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4Third-party services

NumIQ is built on the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Sign-In (optional account linking) — policies.google.com/privacy

5Data storage & security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure with industry-standard security practices. Access to gameplay data is restricted to your own account — no other player can read or write your personal records. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6Data retention & deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (progress, stats, puzzle history) at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This does not automatically cancel an active Play Store subscription — cancel that separately in the Play Store to stop future billing.

7Children's privacy

NumIQ is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

8Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction — most gameplay data is visible directly in your Profile.
  • Deletion — delete your account and data any time via Settings.
  • Notifications — toggle push notifications off any time in Settings.
  • Ad personalization — manage ad tracking permissions in your device's system settings.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of NumIQ after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
