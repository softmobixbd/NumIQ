Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Effective date: July 29, 2026 · Contact: softmobixbd@gmail.com

In short: NumIQ lets you play as an anonymous guest with no personal information at all, or link a Google account to save your progress across devices. We never sell your data. The third-party services we use (Google AdMob for ads, Google Play Billing for purchases, Firebase for accounts/sync/crash reporting/notifications) each collect limited data described below.

1Who we are

NumIQ is developed and published by SoftMobiXBD (softmobixbd), an independent mobile game studio based in Bangladesh, operating as an individual developer — not a registered company. This Privacy Policy explains how NumIQ ("we," "our," "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app. By using NumIQ, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2Information we collect

Account information. When you play as a guest, we create an anonymous device account with no personal information attached. If you choose to link a Google account, we receive your Google display name, email address, and profile photo URL.

Gameplay data. We store your puzzle solves, streaks, XP, coin balance, badges, IQ score, difficulty preferences, and puzzle history so your progress carries across sessions and devices.

Puzzle reports. If you report a puzzle as incorrect or confusing, we store the reason you selected, any optional details you type, and a snapshot of that puzzle so our team can review it. Reports are visible only to you and to us.

Push notification token. If you enable notifications, we store a device token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used only to send you gameplay-related reminders (daily challenge reminders, streak warnings).

Purchase information. If you buy NumIQ Pro or a coin pack, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made.

Advertising & crash data. If you're on the free tier, our ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, subject to your device's ad tracking permissions. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us an anonymized crash report (stack trace, device model, OS version) containing no personal information.

3How we use your information

  • To operate core gameplay: puzzles, progress tracking, streaks, leaderboards, and Battle Mode
  • To personalize your daily puzzle difficulty and recommended feed
  • To review puzzle reports and, where a report is confirmed, to grant the reward tied to it
  • To send optional gameplay reminders, if notifications are enabled
  • To process and deliver in-app purchases
  • To serve and measure advertising for non-Pro users
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4Third-party services

NumIQ is built on the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Sign-In (optional account linking) — policies.google.com/privacy

These services are provided by Google LLC. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy documentation.

5Your consent choices (GDPR / EEA & UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app shows a consent dialog (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ads are loaded. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads; NumIQ works fully either way. You can revisit this choice any time the consent requirement applies by reinstalling the app or through your device's ad settings.

6Data storage & security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure with industry-standard security practices. Access to gameplay data is restricted to your own account — no other player can read or write your personal records. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7Bangladesh data protection law

As a Bangladesh-based developer, we aim to honor the principles of Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 — in particular, that you own your personal data and that we should only collect, store, or use it with your knowledge and for the purposes described in this policy. Some categories of personal data under that Act are expected to be stored within Bangladesh; NumIQ's data is currently hosted on Google Firebase's regional infrastructure (Singapore), the same setup common to most Bangladeshi apps built on global cloud platforms. If this affects you and you'd like more detail on where a specific piece of your data lives, contact us below.

8Data retention & deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (progress, stats, puzzle history, reports) at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This does not automatically cancel an active Play Store subscription — cancel that separately in the Play Store to stop future billing.

9Children's privacy

NumIQ is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

10Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction — most gameplay data is visible directly in your Profile.
  • Deletion — delete your account and data any time via Settings.
  • Notifications — toggle push notifications off any time in Settings.
  • Ad personalization — manage ad tracking permissions in your device's system settings, or via the EEA/UK consent dialog described above.
  • Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA, Bangladesh's PDPA, and similar laws), you may have further rights to access, correct, or delete data we or our third-party providers hold. Contact us and we'll help direct your request.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of NumIQ after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
